# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — VYNL

## 1. Vision
VYNL aims to revolutionize music discovery by merging **explainable AI** with **community-driven social listening**. It replaces the fragmented experience of using multiple apps for streaming and discussion with a single, transparent, and collaborative platform.

## 2. Target Audience
- **The Active Discoverer:** Users bored of "black-box" playlists who want to understand *why* music is suggested and have more control over their curation.
- **The Social Listener:** Music fans who enjoy discussing tracks, sharing real-time experiences, and participating in genre-specific communities.
- **The Collaborative Curator:** Groups (friends, coworkers) who want to build and listen to playlists together without synchronization issues.

## 3. Core Pillars
- **Transparent Recommendations:** Every AI suggestion comes with a human-readable reason (e.g., "Matches your lo-fi vibe").
- **Active Curation:** Move from passive listening to "seeding" sessions where the AI acts as a co-pilot, not a pilot.
- **Social Integration:** Native discussion hubs and threaded song comments to keep the conversation where the music is.
- **Real-time Collaboration:** Conflict-free, multi-user playlist editing.

## 4. Business Objectives
- **User Trust:** Build loyalty by demystifying the recommendation engine.
- **Engagement:** Increase session length through interactive curation and native community features.
- **Viral Growth:** Leverage collaborative playlists as a natural invite mechanism.
- **Competitive Edge:** Provide studio-grade audio (EAC-3) and deep personalization (lyric templates) for power users.

## 5. Key Features (MVP)
- AI recommendation engine with "Why" explanations.
- Real-time collaborative playlists using CRDTs.
- Genre-based discussion channels and threaded song comments.
- Adaptive bitrate streaming via Telegram-backed storage.
- Customizable synced lyric displays.

## 6. Success Metrics
- **Discovery Rate:** % of recommended tracks that are liked or added to playlists.
- **Retention:** User return rate driven by community engagement in discussion hubs.
- **Collaboration Index:** Number of multi-user playlists and concurrent edit sessions.
- **Session Duration:** Average time spent listening and interacting with social features.
